# Rubric used by the raters (video-grounded check, 0-5 per report)

5    Exact: the described glitch is clearly visible; object, behaviour and what is wrong are all correct.
4    Mostly correct: the right glitch, with a minor detail wrong or missing.
3    Partly correct: right object/event, but what goes wrong is described inaccurately or exaggerated.
2    Weak: something related is in the clip, but the report mostly misdescribes it, or it reports a minor side oddity instead of the clip's glitch.
1    Barely: only vague overlap (e.g. the right character, but invented behaviour).
0    Wrong: not in the clip at all, or normal gameplay described as a glitch.
Judge the description against what you see. Spans are where the system claims it happens — the glitch may also be elsewhere in the clip; frame numbers in the text can be ignored.
